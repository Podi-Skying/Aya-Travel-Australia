## 旅遊規劃小測驗 (owner and share links only; it starts from Kai's residence)

- **Steps** (progress dots, back navigation, 44px targets):
  0. **起點:** the home base (shown as 「住處」 to share viewers).
  1. **區域:** single choice among fixed areas that have places in the trip data. Each card shows its photo, the place count, whether a car is needed, and whether it is too far for a day (East Coast, North, Cradle).
  2. **景點:** multi-select photo grid built on React Bits ChromaGrid (JS-CSS), vendored with a local selection extension: `selected`/`onToggle` render cards as `aria-pressed` buttons with a vermilion check badge. A running count is shown. The grid uses 3 columns, or 2 on phones.
  3. **交通:** 開車 or 大眾運輸, plus a departure time of 08:00, 09:00 or 10:00.
  4. **結果:**
     - Stops are ordered nearest-first from home and return home.
     - The map is the day map: cached road legs where known, otherwise straight; the 住處 landmark is at the start and end.
     - The stop list shows estimated drive times and the return time, and the total cost is worked out from activity prices.
     - Buttons: 重新規劃, and for the owner 「加入某一天」 (op `addQuizPlan`: pool places join the chosen day as pending, in one undoable version).
- **Calls:** the quiz makes no Google calls. Legs are estimated from straight-line distance and roads are read from the cache only.
