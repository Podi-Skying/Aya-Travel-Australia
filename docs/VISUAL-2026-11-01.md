## What changed

- **Day:** photo cover with the date and title on it; icon tile when the day has no photo. Weather, rain backup, cost, stay, driving, driver and reminders are chips, and the full sentence opens on tap. Round-photo route strip that scrolls to a stop. Main sights lead with a 16:9 photo with time and status on it. Other stops are one-line rows. Legs are slim connectors with a navigation icon. Whole-day navigation is a chip. Owner actions moved into a ⋯ menu.
- **Plans:** intro removed, shorter captions, chips for nights, dates, driving and cost; long text behind 更多.
- **Info:** boarding-pass flights, stay cards, weather tiles for the whole trip, budget bars. Checklist, official alerts, aurora and rules are collapsed.
- **Quiz:** intro removed, shorter region labels, picture choices for transport.
- **Map:** shorter popups with an icon link; legend as glyphs.
- **Gallery:** instructions became a hint that disappears after first use.
- **Header and footer:** public-view notice and sync time became icon chips.
- **Home:** shorter caption; English stop names hidden on phones.

Full sentences stay one tap away. Icons carry labels for screen readers in all three languages. Bracketed status in a stop's name, such as 「（考慮中・未預訂）」, is hidden from the title and shown as an icon; the full name is under 更多. Trip data was not edited.

## Verified on the live site (iPhone 13 emulation, guest)

- The gallery hint is gone after first touch and stays gone after reload.
- No page errors. The only Google request is the web font; no Maps or Places call.
