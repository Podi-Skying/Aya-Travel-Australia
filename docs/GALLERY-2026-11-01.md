# Gallery on phones, 2026-11-01

Kai: 「畫廊功能在手機使用上不順，會瞬間變換」.

## What caused the jumps

1. Photos load in batches. Each batch changed the list, and the field re-picked every plane's photo, so all visible photos swapped at once.
2. A recycled plane showed its old photo until the new file arrived, often a full-size file of up to 1.6 MB.
3. During a drag the camera moved twice: with the finger and with its velocity.
4. The axis was chosen on every move, so a diagonal swipe jittered. A second finger or a cancelled touch caused jumps.
5. Release speed came from one sample, so a jitter could throw the field far.
6. After a backward flick, the idle drift reversed it abruptly.

## What changed

- A plane keeps its photo until it recycles out of sight. The new photo is decoded first, then eases in over 200 ms. The next 12 are fetched ahead.
- One finger and one axis, locked after 10 px, then 1:1. Release speed comes from the last 100 ms, is 0 after a pause, and is capped.
- The drift returns 1.5 s after the last touch, in the same direction.
- 24 planes instead of 36, with 500 px Commons images.
- A tapped photo grows from its place into a sheet above the tab bar, with a dimmed background. Swipe down, tap outside, Esc or 關閉 closes it along the same path. Focus moves in and back. Reduced motion cross-fades.
- On phones the field fills the screen under the top lines.

## Verified live (iPhone 13 emulation, CDP touch events)

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Photo swaps while visible, during loading and after swipes | 0 of 30 |
| Diagonal swipe, depth per step | 0, then a steady 22 in one direction |
| Second finger | no movement |
| Cancelled touch | stops, no fling |
| Frame time at 4× CPU throttle in headless Chrome | median 17 ms, max 17 ms |
| Tap | sheet grows from the photo (134 → 174 px at 40 ms) and focus lands on 關閉 |
| Swipe down | follows the finger 1:1, then closes |

Not tested on a real iPhone; headless frame times may be better than a real device. The optional sideways swipe in the sheet was not built.
