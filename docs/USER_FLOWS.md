# User Flows

## Owner
1. Log in and open the trip.
2. Review itinerary, data freshness, feasibility, and warnings.
3. Generate a plan or request changes.
4. Review schedule conflicts, assumptions, costs, sources, and alternatives.
5. Accept the whole proposal or selected items.
6. Review the rechecked itinerary and budget.
7. Save/share a view-only link.
8. Review aggregate interest counts.
9. Restore an earlier version if needed; restoration creates a new version.
10. Save core trip details for offline use.

## Visitor
1. Open a shared link without an account.
2. Review activities, transport, estimated costs, sources, and warnings.
3. Submit interest for an activity/date with a nickname.
4. Edit or withdraw the response where the browser/device record permits.

## Disruption
1. A major alert or material data change is detected, or the owner opens the trip and a warning is due.
2. Identify affected activities and dependent timing, transport, and budget.
3. Show the source, timestamp, impact, and verification status.
4. Suggest alternatives or rescheduling.
5. The owner decides whether to apply the changes; the original itinerary remains available.
