# AI, Itinerary, and Budget Rules

## AI inputs and output
Use trip dates, starting/accommodation location, budget and currency, interests, transport options, group size, activity priorities, and known constraints.

A proposed itinerary should include:
- Activity order and suggested times, duration, and known opening-hour constraints.
- Transport mode and estimated door-to-door travel time, with components separated where possible.
- Itemised estimated costs, units, assumptions, and trip totals.
- Alternatives and the reason for recommending them.
- Sources, last-checked time where available, and verification/uncertainty labels.
- Cross-day checks for travel, accommodation, budget, and schedule conflicts.

## Priority and feasibility
- **Must-do:** do not silently remove. Explain conflicts and request a decision or offer options.
- **Preferred:** prioritise when compatible with dates, interests, feasibility, cost, and travel time.
- **Replaceable:** first candidates for substitution when weather, transport, or timing changes.

Feasibility labels:
- **Verified:** key constraints have adequate, current-enough supporting information and no major conflict was found.
- **Partially verified:** the plan appears reasonable, but important details remain unconfirmed.
- **Not verified:** key information is missing or a material conflict remains.

These labels describe available evidence; they are not a guarantee that travel will proceed as planned. A known closure or impossible connection must not be described as executable. It may remain visible in the original plan with a prominent warning.

## Changes and disruptions
1. Preserve the original itinerary.
2. Identify the affected activity and downstream timing, transport, and budget impacts.
3. Check safety and practical feasibility first.
4. Suggest a nearby alternative or rescheduling, with source and uncertainty details.
5. Apply changes only after owner confirmation, then revalidate the affected plan.

## Daily pace and transport
- Three to four major activities per day is a flexible target, not a requirement.
- Reduce activity load for long transfers, ferries, demanding hikes, and disruptions.
- Mix driving, ferry, public transport, and walking based on the actual route and date.
- Do not assume a friend is available to drive until the exact date and time window are confirmed.
- Proposed guideline, awaiting confirmation: ordinary driving days aim for about 2–3 hours of total driving; longer transfer days may exceed this if activity load and rest breaks are adjusted.
- Check current official road and park alerts before treating a route as feasible.

## Accommodation
**Proposed policy:** compare staying overnight with returning to the current base, considering total travel time, fatigue, transport access, accommodation cost, and the next day's route. Show price, availability date, and cancellation terms only when supported by current data. Never imply a suggested property is booked.

## Budget calculation
For each item, show amount/range and currency, cost basis, people/days/nights used, status (estimate/quote/confirmed), source and update time where available, and exclusions/assumptions.

Include transport, accommodation, food, tickets/activities, and other relevant costs. Show whole-trip totals and useful daily, category, group, and per-person views. Do not apply a universal budget-error percentage.

Trip-specific rules:
- UTAS Hobart Apartments is AUD $0 for the owner and Aya only on nights they stay there.
- Budget other accommodation separately.
- Split fuel, parking, ferry, and similar shared vehicle costs among the participants in that driving leg, not automatically across the entire group.
