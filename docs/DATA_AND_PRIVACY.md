# Data, Privacy, and Security

## Source quality and freshness
Source priority:
1. Official attraction, transport, government, road, and national park sources.
2. Relevant operators and booking providers.
3. Reputable secondary sources, clearly labelled when official data is unavailable.

Data groups:
- **Critical alerts:** road/park closures, severe weather, major service disruption.
- **Operational data:** opening hours, transport schedules, prices, booking links.
- **Planning estimates:** travel times, budget ranges, seasonal advice.
- **Evergreen content:** attraction descriptions and general travel information.

For important claims, show the source and last-checked/updated time when available. Use appropriate labels such as estimated, confirmed, stale, conflicting, unavailable, or unverified. If sources conflict, flag the conflict for owner review.

Refresh critical alerts first, operational data periodically, and recheck important details before departure where feasible. Exact thresholds, refresh intervals, provider access, licensing, and cost remain TBD.

If a source is unavailable, do not imply the data is live. Retain a last-known value only with a stale/last-sync label and provide an official link for manual checking.

## Privacy and access
- Owner login required; visitors need no account.
- Shared links are view-only for the itinerary and can be deactivated by the owner. No expiry is planned for the first version.
- The exact accommodation address is hidden by default; name/area may be shown. Private transport details may be hidden.
- A shared link can reveal travel dates and activity patterns even when the exact address is hidden. Review the content before publishing.
- Interest responses collect only the activity/date and nickname unless later requirements justify more.
- The owner sees aggregate counts, not a list of individual voters.
- Browser/device-based duplicate protection is imperfect. Consider rate limits or temporary restrictions; add CAPTCHA only if needed.

## Offline copies and deletion
The owner can delete itinerary data stored by the service. This cannot recall screenshots, copied information, or already-downloaded offline copies. The exact handling of cached copies and account recovery remains TBD.
